Mii Casa (su casa)

A shared USC dorm, modeled 1:1 in Blender and built by hand in two weeks, designed for anyone to come and feel at home.

Role
Designer, builder, project lead
Timeline
Months of planning, 2 weeks of construction
Tools
Blender, sketchbook, IYA Creator Studio
Materials
IKEA furniture and lamps, Home Depot lumber
01

Overview

Mii Casa is my redesign of the IYA upperclassmen's dorm at USC, and my biggest physical project to date. It's the result of months of planning and two weeks of construction, funded entirely by the money I made from my summer internship.

The goal was a space with an open door: somewhere people could show up to chill or study without an invitation. It has since become the new IYA spot, and anyone is welcome.

02

Measure and model

I began by taking measurements of the IYA upperclassmen's dorm last semester, sketching every wall and piece of furniture by hand. From there I modeled the space and furniture 1:1 in Blender, starting from the housing floor plan, so every design decision would hold up against the real room.

Hand-measured sketches of the room and every piece of furniture.

03

Design in 3D

Once the constraints were in place, the creativity began. I explored several different layouts and decor options, iterating on wood slat walls, paper lanterns, and plants until I was happy with the renders.

04

Render vs. reality

Because the model was built to scale, the finished room lands close to the renders.

05

Sourcing

The minute I landed in LA, the clock started ticking; I had only a couple weeks before schoolwork would take over. I roped friends into the adventure and bought my materials: IKEA lamps, chairs, and a table, plus wooden boards from Home Depot.

06

Fabrication

The IKEA pieces went up quickly. Cutting the wood took up the bulk of my time: over roughly five days I spent hours in the IYA Creator Studio cutting huge boards into three-inch slats. The rest was a matter of synthesis, setup, and final touches.

07

Built together

This was far from a solo project. Along the way I enlisted the help of different friends who brought the entire dorm together, and the making of Mii Casa left me with so many fond memories.

08

Outcome

Mii Casa runs on an open door policy. If anyone wants to come chill or study, they just show up. It was designed specifically for anyone to come and feel at home, and it has become a gathering spot for the IYA community and beyond.
