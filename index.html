The space

01 · Planning

Modeled 1:1 before a single board was cut

As my biggest physical project to date, Mii casa is the result of months of planning and two weeks of construction. I began by taking measurements of the IYA upperclassmen's dorm last semester and modeling the space and furniture 1:1 in Blender. Once the constraints were in place, the creativity began. I messed around with several different potential layouts and decor until I was happy with the renders that came out.

02 · Building

A couple of weeks on the clock

The minute I landed in LA, the clock started ticking; I had only a couple weeks before my schoolwork would consume my time. I roped friends into the adventure and began purchasing my materials: IKEA lamps, chairs, table and wooden boards from Home Depot. The entirety of this project was funded by the money I made from my summer internship.

Then the real work began. I was able to easily set up all of the pieces from IKEA, however, cutting the wood took up the bulk of my time. Over roughly 5 days I would spend hours in the IYA creator studio cutting these huge boards into three-inch slats. The rest was a matter of synthesis, set up, and final touches.

03 · The people

Far from a solo project

This however was far from a solo project. Along the way I would enlist the help of different friends who brought the entire dorm together. I hold so many fond memories thanks to just the making of Mii casa, and as the school year goes on I can't wait to make countless more. We have an open door policy so if anyone wants to come to chill or study they just show up; it's becoming the new IYA spot but anyone is welcome.

Apologies if this goes more in depth than was necessary, I'm still a little high off the fumes of her completion. I hope this can at least give a decent look into the space and its brief history. As any student, we would be ecstatic to host Travis, but honestly we're just happy to have people in the space; it was designed specifically for anyone to come and feel at home. This is in fact an invitation in a way, for you or anyone you know.

Thanks for the consideration and I'll see you in class next Tuesday,
